Sommelle is a support tool inspired by cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), which the European and American guidelines recommend as first-line care. Sommelle has not been clinically evaluated and does not replace a doctor.

Fully open source (MPL-2.0), free, ad-free, no subscription, no account, and 100% offline: it does not even request the Internet permission. Your sleep data never leaves your phone.

A 6-WEEK PROGRAM
• Starting questions: the app checks whether reducing your time in bed suits your situation (possible sleep apnea, bipolar disorder, epilepsy, pregnancy, jobs that demand constant alertness, distress) and points you to a suitable path or to a doctor.
• One week of observation: a sleep diary filled in within twenty seconds each morning, without typing, to find your starting point (time in bed, time asleep, share of bed time spent sleeping).
• A transition step: your starting figures and an explanation of the method before you begin.
• Weeks 1 to 6: time in bed reduced to what you actually sleep (never under 6 h), a fixed rise time, tips for nights awake, and exercises to calm the thoughts that keep insomnia going. Each week, your window is adjusted from your sleep diary.
• A review at the end: your first nights compared with your latest nights, based on your sleep diary.

GOOD TO KNOW
• During the first weeks, sleepiness may increase. If you struggle to stay awake, do not drive and do not use dangerous machinery.
• If you take a sleeping pill or an anti-anxiety medicine, do not stop it or change the dose without your doctor's advice.

TOOLS
• Sleep diary inspired by the Consensus Sleep Diary.
• Very dim "I can't sleep" night screen.
• PDF and CSV export for your doctor.
• Encrypted backup of your data, kept by you.
• No trackers, no data resale.
